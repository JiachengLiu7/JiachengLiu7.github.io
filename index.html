01 / ABOUT

From industrial systems
to intelligent machines.

I’m a Robotics MSc student at the University of Manchester, with an Industrial Engineering background from Hefei University of Technology.

My work connects perception algorithms with physical engineering systems. I have developed a skeleton-based fall detection system, modelled manufacturing logistics, and gained engineering experience across automotive and industrial environments.

I am currently involved in tactile sensing and robotic grasping research. My interests include robot perception, feedback-driven manipulation and system integration.
